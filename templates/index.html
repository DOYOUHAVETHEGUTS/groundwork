Vendor quotes

3 required

Three quotes are required — fewer is an automatic fail. Itemize each one so the bids can be compared line by line, and mark the vendor you're selecting.

Not always the cheapest. Explain the best value all-in: scope covered, lead time, warranty, support, compliance, total cost.

Cost build-up

Quantity × unit cost for every line, then contingency. NPV and payback are prepared by Finance with the division template.

Corporate Real Estate

CRE-driven requests (facilities, buildings, leases, space) must use the CRE template. You have to declare either way.

    Settings

    Stored server-side in data/settings.json. Never sent to the browser.

    Some newer models (incl. current Claude) reject a custom value — the app retries automatically without it if that happens.

    Finance escalation

    Who hears about a request that is still below the bar after two rounds of guided questions. A finance email entered on the request overrides this default.

    Example images & budget
